HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS
A / D or ← →Move
W / ↑ / SpaceJump (spike approach or block)
J / ZSpike in the air · Serve (toss, then hit)
J + forwardStraight-Line Spike (deep)
J + backCross-Cut Spike (sharp angle)
J + S / ↓Soft Feint Drop
J + L / CCurve Spike (late dipping drop)
K / XArm your Special (gauge full)
S / ↓ on groundDive dig
P / Esc · M · VPause · Mute · Voices
Receiving & setting are automatic when the ball reaches your arms on the ground. Get under it. Your teammate sets you high toward wherever you're standing. A gold ring shows where the set is going.
Spiking: jump so your hand meets the ball near the top of your jump, then press J. Hold a direction for the shot type. The dotted line shows where it'll land.
Blocking: stand near the net and jump when the opponent swings. Your Block stat and timing decide stuff blocks, soft blocks, and tools.
Specials charge from touches, digs, and points. Press K to arm one. Your next attack (or block or dig, depending on the move) becomes a super move.
Power-up gems float over the court. Touch one (jump for the high ones) to grab it: ⚡ Lightning Sprint (speed for 7s), 🪽 High Fly Jump (jump for 7s), 🎯 Focus Zone (your next point counts double), ☄️ Meteor Spike (your next attack is a meteor).
Coins come from points and wins. Spend them in the Locker Room on cosmetics for any player.
